For my first milestone, I finished creating the car chasis. I did this by following a tutorial on YouTube, and then I had to hot glue battery and a bluetooth module onto the chasis. I also learned how motor drivers worked, and programmed the car's wheels to turn backwards and forwards for a set amount of time. I also finished setting up the accelerometer portion of the project, which is supposed to track the movement of your hand. However, I am running into issues with my accelerometer because it is not picking up any of my hand motions. I think this may be because I am using a different Arduino, and so the code and the pins may funciton differently. For the next milestone, I want to try to get the accelerometer fully functioning so it actually detects movement, and program the car's wheels to move depending on how my hand does.
